Medical Disclaimer & System Objectives

• This system is designed to promote ergonomic stretching exercises during work hours for individuals at risk of office syndrome, focusing on the cervical spine, trapezius, shoulders, and thoracic/lumbar regions.
• It does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional medical treatment. If you experience sharp pain, radiating arm numbness, dizziness, or bone/joint injuries, stop using the system immediately and consult a healthcare professional.
• All video processing is performed entirely client-side. No image data is recorded or transmitted externally, ensuring strict data privacy compliance.
